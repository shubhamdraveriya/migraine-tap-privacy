# Migraine Tap — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 9 November 2026
**Developer:** Shubham D
**Contact:** shubhamdraveriya@gmail.com

Migraine Tap is a migraine diary for Wear OS watches and Android phones. You log migraine attacks on your watch and
review them on your phone. This policy explains what the app handles, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

## The short version

- Your migraine diary stays **on your own watch and phone**. We do not run servers. We only receive something if you
  choose to email it to us, such as the optional anonymous stats described below.
- The app has **no accounts, no ads, no analytics SDKs and no tracking**. The only usage data we receive is the optional anonymous stats you choose to email us (see below). Neither the watch app nor the phone app requests
  internet access.
- If you allow it, the phone app **reads** your sleep and heart rate from Health Connect to show context next to each
  attack. It never writes to Health Connect.
- We do **not sell, share or rent** your data to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

## What the app handles

| Data | Where it comes from | Where it is kept | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Attack start and end times, severity (mild / moderate / severe), medication names you log, your time-zone offset | You, on the watch | On the watch until delivered to your phone, and on the phone | To build your migraine history |
| Triggers and notes you add | You, on the phone | On the phone | To give your history context |
| Medication list (up to 4 names) | You, on the watch | On the watch | One-tap medication logging |
| Sleep sessions and heart-rate readings around each attack | Health Connect, **only with your permission** | Read when you open or refresh an attack; not stored by us elsewhere | To show sleep the night before and average heart rate during an attack |

The app does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location or advertising ID.

## How your watch and phone talk to each other

Logged attacks travel from your watch to your phone through **Google Play services' Wearable Data Layer**, the
standard Android mechanism for watch-to-phone sync. This normally happens directly over Bluetooth. When Bluetooth isn't
available but both devices are online, Google Play services may relay the data through Google's servers to deliver it.
That transfer is handled by Google under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Once your phone
has stored an entry, the app deletes it from the sync channel.

## Health Connect

If you grant permission, Migraine Tap reads **Sleep** and **Heart rate** records from Health Connect. Those are
the only health data types it requests, and it has read access only. It reads them while you are using the app, to
show context for an attack. It does not read health data in the background, and it never writes or changes Health
Connect data.

Migraine Tap's use of information received from Health Connect follows the
["Health Connect by Android Permissions" policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9888170),
including its Limited Use requirements:

- Health Connect data is used only to show sleep and heart-rate context next to your attacks, inside the app.
- It is not transferred to anyone, sold, or used for advertising, and it is not used to determine credit-worthiness
  or for lending.
- It is not used to train AI or machine-learning models.
- No person reads your data.

You can withdraw access at any time in **Settings → Health Connect → App permissions → Migraine Tap**.

## Sharing and exporting

The app does not share your data. When **you** tap **Export**, the app creates a CSV file of your attacks
(including sleep and heart-rate values, if shown) and opens Android's share sheet. Where that file goes, for example to
your doctor by email, is your choice. Once shared, it is handled by the app or person you sent it to.

## Sharing anonymous stats (optional)

The phone app has a **Share my anonymous stats** button. It first shows you the exact text, then opens your
own email app with a pre-filled message to shubhamdraveriya@gmail.com. Nothing is sent unless you tap send in your
email app. The message contains only counts: the app version, number of attacks (in total and in the last 30 days),
number of days with attacks, days since your first attack, whether a second attack was logged within 30 days of the
first, and how many attacks have a severity or medication logged. It does
**not** contain dates, times, notes, triggers, medication names or any health data.

We use these counts only to decide whether Migraine Tap is worth developing further. Your email address and the
message are kept in the developer's mailbox and are not shared or used for marketing. To have a message deleted,
email us.

## Deleting your data

You can delete everything Migraine Tap holds about you at any time:

- **On the watch and phone:** uninstall the app, or open **Settings → Apps → Migraine Tap → Storage → Clear storage** on each device. This permanently deletes the attack log, notes and medication names on that device.
- **Health Connect data:** the app doesn't store any Health Connect data itself. To stop access, open **Settings → Health Connect → App permissions → Migraine Tap** and turn off sleep and heart rate.
- **Anonymous stats you emailed us:** email shubhamdraveriya@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my stats" and include the address you sent from. We will delete those messages.

Your data is never held on our servers, so there is nothing else to delete.
## Storage, security and retention

- Data is stored in the app's private storage on your watch and phone, which other apps can't read.
- Device backup is turned off for both apps, so the diary isn't copied to cloud backups.
- Data is kept until you delete it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in **Settings → Apps → Migraine Tap
  → Storage**, permanently deletes everything on that device. Do this on both the watch and the phone to remove all
  data. We cannot recover deleted data, because we never had a copy.

## Your choices and rights

Because your data never reaches us, you control it directly on your devices: view it in the app, export it, revoke
Health Connect access, or delete it as described above. Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights over
your personal data, for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the EU/UK GDPR or US state
privacy laws. If you have questions or want to exercise a right, contact us at shubhamdraveriya@gmail.com.

## Children

Migraine Tap is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly
collect data from them.

## Not medical advice

Migraine Tap is a personal diary. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.
Always talk to a qualified healthcare professional about your health.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or
on its Google Play listing before the change takes effect.
